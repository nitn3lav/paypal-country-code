# Country codes (/reference/country-codes)

Use these country codes when you specify country in API requests and account configurations.



The REST APIs support many countries for PayPal account payments if you have a business account. For general country-specific offerings and limitations, see [PayPal Offerings Worldwide](https://www.paypal.com/webapps/mpp/country-worldwide) and visit your country-specific site.
